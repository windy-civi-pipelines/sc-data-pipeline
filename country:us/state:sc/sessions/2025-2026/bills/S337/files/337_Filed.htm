Printed 3/11/25--S.                                                                            [SEC 3/12/2025 5:28 PM] Read the first time February 12, 2025 ________ A bill TO AMEND THE SOUTH CAROLINA CODE OF LAWS BY AMENDING SECTION 50-13-640, RELATING TO THE POSSESSION OF BLUE CATFISH, SO AS TO PROVIDE FOR A DAILY LIMIT OF SEVENTY-FIVE BLUE CATFISH IN STATE WATERWAYS OTHER THAN IN LAKE MARION, LAKE MOULTRIE, AND THE UPPER REACH OF THE SANTEE RIVER. Amend Title To Conform Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: SECTION 1.  Section 50-13-640(B) of the S.C. Code is amended to read: (B) It is unlawful to take possess more than twenty-five blue catfish (Ictalurus furcatus) a day in Lake Marion, Lake Moultrie, and the upper reach of the Santee River, and it is unlawful to possess  more than one-hundred-fifty blue catfish a day in all other state waterways. SECTION 2.  Section 50-13-640(A) of the S.C. Code is amended to read: (A) It is unlawful to possess more than two blue catfish (Ictalurus furcatus) greater than thirty-two inches in length in any one day in Lake Marion, Lake Moultrie, or the upper reach of the Santee River, and the Congaree and Wateree rivers, and all other state waterways. SECTION 3.  This act takes effect upon approval by the Governor. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on March 12, 2025 at 5:28 PM ================================================================================ Raw Text: 2025-2026 Bill 337: Blue Catfish - South Carolina Legislature Online South Carolina General Assembly126th Session, 2025-2026Download This Bill in Microsoft Word FormatIndicates Matter StrickenIndicates New MatterS. 337STATUS INFORMATIONGeneral BillSponsors: Senator ReichenbachDocument Path: SFGF-0018BC25.docxIntroduced in the Senate on February 12, 2025Introduced in the House on March 20, 2025Last Amended on March 11, 2025 Agriculture, Natural Resources and Environmental AffairsSummary: Blue CatfishHISTORY OF LEGISLATIVE ACTIONS Date Body Action Description with journal page number 2/12/2025 Senate Introduced and read first time (Senate Journal-page 3) 2/12/2025 Senate Referred to Committee on Fish, Game and Forestry (Senate Journal-page 3) 3/5/2025 Senate Committee report: Favorable with amendment Fish, Game and Forestry (Senate Journal-page 20) 3/11/2025 Senate Committee Amendment Adopted (Senate Journal-page 27) 3/11/2025 Senate Read second time (Senate Journal-page 27) 3/11/2025 Senate Roll call Ayes-41 Nays-0 (Senate Journal-page 27) 3/12/2025 Scrivener's error corrected 3/12/2025 Senate Read third time and sent to House (Senate Journal-page 10) 3/20/2025 House Introduced and read first time (House Journal-page 21) 3/20/2025 House Referred to Committee on Agriculture, Natural Resources and Environmental Affairs (House Journal-page 21) View the latest legislative information at the websiteVERSIONS OF THIS BILL02/12/202503/05/202503/05/2025-A03/06/202503/11/202503/12/2025 Indicates Matter Stricken Indicates New Matter Committee Amendment Adopted March 11, 2025 S. 337 Introduced by Senator Reichenbach S.
