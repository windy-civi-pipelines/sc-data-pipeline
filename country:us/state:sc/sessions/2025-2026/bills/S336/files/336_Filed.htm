Read the first time February 12, 2025 ________ A bill TO AMEND THE SOUTH CAROLINA CODE OF LAWS BY AMENDING SECTION 2-19-90, RELATING TO APPROVAL OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY IN JOINT SESSION, SO AS TO SET THE FIRST WEDNESDAY OF March FOR THE ELECTIONS OF JUDGES BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY. Amend Title To Conform Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of South Carolina: SECTION 1.  Section 2-19-90 of the S.C. Code is amended to read: Section 2-19-90. The General Assembly shall meet in joint session for the election of judges on the first Wednesday of March at noon in the House of Representatives.  The date and time for the joint session shall be set by concurrent resolution upon the recommendation of the Judicial Merit Selection Commission.  The Chairman of the Judicial Merit Selection Commission shall announce the commission's nominees for each judicial race, and no further nominating or seconding speeches shall be allowed by members of the General Assembly.  In order to be elected, a candidate must receive a majority of the vote of the members of the General Assembly voting in joint session. SECTION 2.  Section 2-19-80(E) of the S.C. Code is amended to read: (E) A period of at least twenty-two days must elapse between the date of the commission's nominations to the General Assembly and The commission must formally release its report as to the qualifications of its nominees to the General Assembly not less than twenty-three days before the date the General Assembly conducts the election for these judgeships. SECTION 3.  This act takes effect on July 1, 2025. ----XX---- This web page was last updated on March 20, 2025 at 12:24 PM ================================================================================ Raw Text: 2025-2026 Bill 336: Judicial Elections - South Carolina Legislature Online South Carolina General Assembly126th Session, 2025-2026Download This Bill in Microsoft Word FormatIndicates Matter StrickenIndicates New MatterS. 336STATUS INFORMATIONGeneral BillSponsors: Senators Alexander, Massey and RankinDocument Path: SR-0018CEM25.docxIntroduced in the Senate on February 12, 2025Introduced in the House on March 27, 2025Last Amended on March 20, 2025 JudiciarySummary: Judicial ElectionsHISTORY OF LEGISLATIVE ACTIONS Date Body Action Description with journal page number 2/12/2025 Senate Introduced and read first time (Senate Journal-page 3) 2/12/2025 Senate Referred to Committee on Judiciary (Senate Journal-page 3) 2/27/2025 Senate Referred to Subcommittee: Elliott (ch), Cash, Tedder, Devine, Fernandez, Kennedy, Walker 3/12/2025 Senate Committee report: Favorable Judiciary (Senate Journal-page 8) 3/20/2025 Senate Amended (Senate Journal-page 11) 3/20/2025 Senate Read second time (Senate Journal-page 11) 3/20/2025 Senate Roll call Ayes-40 Nays-0 (Senate Journal-page 11) 3/25/2025 Senate Read third time and sent to House (Senate Journal-page 11) 3/27/2025 House Introduced and read first time (House Journal-page 20) 3/27/2025 House Referred to Committee on Judiciary (House Journal-page 20) View the latest legislative information at the websiteVERSIONS OF THIS BILL02/12/202503/12/202503/20/2025 Indicates Matter Stricken Indicates New Matter Amended March 20, 2025 S. 336 Introduced by Senators Alexander, Massey and Rankin S.
